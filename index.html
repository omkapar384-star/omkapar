About the City

Where history meets devotion

Janakpur, also known as Janakpurdham, is one of Nepal's most important religious and cultural destinations. Located in Dhanusha District of Madhesh Province, the city is closely associated with the ancient kingdom of Mithila and the Ramayana tradition.

The city is famous for the magnificent Janaki Mandir, sacred ponds, colorful festivals and beautiful Mithila artwork.

  • ✦ Home of the famous Janaki Mandir
  • ✦ Rich Mithila art and culture
  • ✦ Sacred ponds and historic temples
  • ✦ Famous festivals including Vivah Panchami

Places to Explore

Discover some of the most beautiful and culturally important places around Janakpurdham.

Janaki Mandir

The magnificent temple dedicated to Goddess Sita is the heart of Janakpurdham and one of Nepal's most celebrated religious landmarks.

🛕 Sacred Temple

Ram Mandir

A historic pagoda-style temple near the central sacred areas of Janakpur, associated with Lord Ram and the city's spiritual heritage.

🕉️ Heritage

Ganga Sagar

One of Janakpur's best-known sacred ponds, surrounded by ghats, temples and beautiful spaces for peaceful walks and religious rituals.

🌊 Sacred Pond

Dhanush Sagar

A significant sacred pond in Janakpur, known for its peaceful waters, religious traditions and beautiful surroundings.

🌅 Nature & Faith

Dhanushadham

Located northeast of Janakpur, Dhanushadham is an important pilgrimage destination associated with the legendary bow of Lord Shiva.

🏹 Pilgrimage

A city full of color, art & tradition

Janakpur is more than a pilgrimage destination. It is a living center of Mithila culture, where traditional art, music, festivals and architecture remain part of everyday life.
